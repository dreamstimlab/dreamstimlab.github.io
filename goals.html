Dream readout

Starting from big data, and with the power of artificial intelligence, models for decoding dream content from non-invasive brain data will be developed and tested.

These models will then undergo scrutiny with respect to their validity in an iterative, longitudinal fashion, and will be optimized accordingly.

Lucid dreaming

Targets of Lucid Dreaming (LD) (concurrent dream awareness during sleep) research can be roughly separated into this state's induction, characterization and application.

dreamstimlab is mainly concerned with LD characterization:
Better characterization of LD itself informs about more targeted factors that help induce this state - thus closing the iterative loop that will likely one day identify how to reliably induce dream lucidity "on button press".

We are agnostic as to whether having LDs is ultimately help- or harmful & consider this a fruitful scientific question in itself. If harmful, we are then be interested to explore ways to engineer this phenonemon towards a more healthy and sustainable state.

In any case, a clear and unbiased characterization of LD is indispensible.

Beyond dreaming

These projects & goals will, in all their audacity, require unconventional solutions to some of the toughest problems in human neuroscience.

Independent of whether they will be ultimately solved or not, intermediate knowledge and general software tools will be applicable to more general questions of understanding brain mechanisms, and made open source to aid the wider neuroscience community.
